AI Personalization · #1 Field Guide

Teach Claude
Your Voice

❦

A style guide that's set up right can make Claude's writing sound like you — and not a robot.

Download PDF ↓ Open PDF in New Tab ↗

Section One

4 Places Your Style Guide Can Live

Global

Profile Preferences

Universal rules for every chat. Best for small habits like "never open with Certainly!"

Best for Voice

Project Instructions

Your main home. Loads automatically every time you open that project.

Samples

Project Knowledge

Upload 10–20 of your best posts. Claude uses these as "what good looks like."

Quick Sketch

Custom Style

A short summary of your voice. Toggle it on per chat as needed.

Section Two

What Goes Inside

In This Order

01

Who You Are

2–3 sentences on your role and what you write about.

02

Positive Voice Rules

5–7 rules written as commands. "Be direct. Vary sentence length."

03

Before / After Examples Highest ROI

3–5 real pairs: bad (AI writing) vs. how you'd actually say it.

04

What to Avoid

Banned phrases and structures. Keep it to 15–25 items max.

05

Self-Check Instruction

"Does any sentence sound like a press release? Rewrite it."

Section Three

Save It as a Markdown (.md) File

Let Claude Write It For You

Use Claude's built-in Skill Creator to auto-generate the style guide from your existing writing. No manual writing required — paste your best posts and let it extract the patterns.

Upload to Project Knowledge

Save the output as a .md file and drop it into your Claude Project. It loads automatically into every conversation in that project.

Section Four

Common Mistakes

×

Banned-word list longer than 25 items — Claude ignores the bottom half.

×

Over-formatting the guide — Claude mirrors your formatting back at you.

×

Guide over 3,000 words → diminishing returns kick in fast.

×

Writing it once and never updating it.

The Feedback Loop

Every 5 posts you publish, paste them back in and ask Claude what it learned about your voice. The guide is a living document, not a one-time write.
