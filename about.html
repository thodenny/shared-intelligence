About

Shared Intelligence is a free resource built for everyday professionals who want to use AI practically - without the hype, the paywalls, or the assumption that you're a developer.

There's a new model, a new tool, or a new "everything changes now" headline every week. It's easy to feel like you're already behind. You're not. Most of what gets announced doesn't change what you need to do today.

I'm Thomas Denny - Program Manager at Meta, former Amazon operations leader. I've spent the last two years training colleagues on AI tools and building AI workflows to save real time. I built this to help get you started.
