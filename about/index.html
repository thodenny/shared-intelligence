About

❦

Shared Intelligence is a free resource built for the everyday professional who wants to understand how to use AI practically in their day-to-day life.

It seems like there's a new model, a new tool, or a new "everything changes now" headline every week. It's easy to feel like you're already behind. You're not. Most of what gets announced doesn't change what you need to do today.

I'm Thomas Denny — Program Manager at Meta, former Amazon operations leader. I've spent the last two years training colleagues on AI tools and building AI workflows that save real time. I built this to help get you started.

If this site helped you, share it and let me know!
