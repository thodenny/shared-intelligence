The Landscape

AI Tools Directory

Not an exhaustive list — the tools worth knowing, organized by what they actually do. Every link opens the real thing — no referral codes, no affiliation.

Last updated: April 2026

Chat & Writing

Claude

Anthropic

Best for writing, analysis, and thinking through complex problems. The most nuanced and thoughtful of the major chat models.

ChatGPT

OpenAI

The most widely used AI assistant. Strong general-purpose tool with the largest plugin and integration ecosystem.

Gemini

Google

Google's AI assistant, deeply integrated with Gmail, Docs, and Google Workspace. The obvious choice if you live in Google's ecosystem.

Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft

AI built into Microsoft 365. Best if your work lives in Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams — it works inside those apps natively.

Grok

xAI

Less filtered and more direct than most assistants. Integrated with X and trained on real-time posts. Good for current events and unvarnished takes.

Le Chat

Mistral

Europe's leading AI lab. Fast, capable, and privacy-conscious — a strong alternative to the US giants, particularly popular in enterprise settings.

Meta AI

Meta

Meta's assistant powered by the Muse Spark model. Free, built into WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. The most accessible AI for everyday users.

DeepSeek

DeepSeek

Chinese-built model that shocked the industry with top-tier performance at a fraction of the cost. Strong reasoning capabilities and fully open-source.

Search & Research

Perplexity

Perplexity AI

AI-powered search that cites its sources. The go-to for research, fact-checking, and any question where accuracy and attribution matter.

NotebookLM

Google

Upload your own documents and ask questions about them. Answers are grounded in your sources only — dramatically less hallucination for your own material.

You.com

You.com

AI search engine with source citations and a customizable interface. A solid Perplexity alternative with strong research and coding modes.

Elicit

Elicit

AI research assistant built for academic literature. Upload papers or search across millions of studies — extracts key data, synthesises findings, and spots gaps.

Presentations & Docs

Gamma

Gamma

Generate polished presentations, documents, and webpages from a text prompt in seconds. Cuts deck-building time dramatically.

Tome

Tome

AI-powered presentation tool focused on narrative and storytelling. Great for pitches and decks where the arc of the story matters.

Beautiful.ai

Beautiful.ai

Smart slide design that auto-formats your content as you add it. Less generative than Gamma, more design-assist — good for polished professional decks.

Canva AI

Canva

AI design tools built into Canva's familiar interface. Generate images, write copy, and create presentations without leaving the tool most teams already use.

Voice & Dictation

WisprFlow

Wispr

Dictate anywhere on your computer with AI cleanup. Write emails, Slack messages, and documents by speaking — works in every app.

Otter.ai

Otter.ai

Real-time meeting transcription and AI summaries. Joins your Zoom, Teams, or Meet calls automatically and produces searchable notes.

Fireflies.ai

Fireflies

AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and summarizes calls. Strong on action item extraction and CRM integrations.

Riverside.fm

Riverside

Record high-quality remote podcasts and video interviews, then let AI handle transcription, clipping, and editing. The go-to for creators producing long-form audio and video.

Autonomous Agents

These tools don't just respond — they take action. Give them a goal and they figure out the steps. Still early, but genuinely capable.

Manus

Manus

Fully autonomous AI agent that can browse the web, write code, manage files, and complete complex multi-step tasks independently with minimal hand-holding.

Operator

OpenAI

OpenAI's browser-based agent. Can navigate websites, fill out forms, make reservations, and complete web-based tasks on your behalf.

Claude Computer Use

Anthropic

Lets Claude control a computer — clicking, typing, navigating apps — to complete tasks end-to-end. The most capable general computer agent available today.

Devin

Cognition

Autonomous AI software engineer that plans, codes, tests, and deploys — start to finish. Built for engineering teams that want an AI that works like a junior dev, not an autocomplete.

Image Generation

Midjourney

Midjourney

The gold standard for high-quality AI image generation. Stunning artistic results and a passionate creative community. Accessed via Discord or web.

DALL·E

OpenAI

OpenAI's image generator, available directly inside ChatGPT. Fast, easy to use, and good for quick visuals without leaving your workflow.

Adobe Firefly

Adobe

Adobe's AI image suite, trained on licensed content. The safest choice for commercial use — built into Photoshop, Illustrator, and Express.

Ideogram

Ideogram

Exceptional at generating images with accurate, readable text — a major weakness of most image generators. Great for social graphics and anything with typography.

Stable Diffusion

Stability AI

The open-source image generation model that runs locally or through tools like ComfyUI. Maximum control and privacy — no cloud, no subscription, no limits.

Flux

Black Forest Labs

Next-generation open image model from the creators of Stable Diffusion. Sharper detail, better prompt adherence, and more realistic outputs than earlier open-source models.

Video Generation

Runway

Runway

Professional-grade AI video generation and editing. Widely used by filmmakers and content creators. The most mature video AI platform available.

Pika

Pika Labs

Easy-to-use AI video generation for short clips and social content. Lower barrier to entry than Runway, good starting point for video generation beginners.

Kling AI

Kuaishou

Chinese-built video generator producing some of the most realistic footage available. Particularly strong at lifelike human movement and cinematic quality clips.

Veo

Google

Google DeepMind's video generation model. Capable of generating long, high-quality cinematic clips with strong adherence to prompts and visual consistency.

Coding & Development

GitHub Copilot

GitHub

The most widely used AI coding assistant. Autocompletes code, explains errors, and suggests entire functions inside VS Code and other IDEs.

Cursor

Cursor

AI-first code editor that understands your entire codebase. Faster than Copilot for complex refactors and multi-file changes. Preferred by many engineers.

Replit

Replit

Browser-based coding environment with built-in AI. The best entry point for non-developers who want to build simple tools, automations, or apps without setup.

Windsurf

Codeium

AI-first code editor and Cursor's closest competitor. Agentic coding flows, fast completions, and a free tier that's hard to beat. Rapidly gaining ground in the developer community.

Claude Code

Anthropic

Claude's agentic coding tool that works inside your terminal. Reads, writes, and runs code across your entire codebase — best for engineers who want an agent, not an autocomplete.

Lovable

Lovable

Describe an app and it builds one. Full-stack web apps from a prompt — no code required. Part of the "vibe coding" wave making software accessible to non-developers.

Productivity & Workflow

Notion AI

Notion

AI built into Notion. Summarize meeting notes, draft documents, query your workspace, and generate content without leaving your knowledge base.

Zapier AI

Zapier

Connect apps and automate workflows using plain English. Describe what you want to automate — Zapier's AI builds the workflow. No code required.

Make

Make

Visual automation platform with AI capabilities. More powerful than Zapier for complex, branching workflows. Steeper learning curve, higher ceiling.

n8n

n8n

Open-source workflow automation with native AI nodes. Self-hostable, highly flexible, and free to start — the power user's alternative to Zapier for teams that want full control.

Lindy AI

Lindy

Build AI agents for repetitive tasks — email triage, scheduling, lead follow-up — in plain English. Feels closer to hiring an assistant than configuring software.
